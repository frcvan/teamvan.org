# Competitions

## This Season

We expect 15 to 20 committed members this season. We plan to compete at the Orlando Regional and the Rocket City Regional, and at the FIRST Championship if we qualify.

## How Qualifying for the FIRST Championship Works

Florida uses FIRST's regional model instead of the district model, so our season registration covers one regional event. Teams qualify for the FIRST Championship directly from a regional by performing well in the playoffs, or by winning an award that carries an automatic bid, such as the Regional Impact Award or the Engineering Inspiration Award.

This season we'll be registered for two regionals, the Orlando Regional and the Rocket City Regional, so we get two chances to qualify.
