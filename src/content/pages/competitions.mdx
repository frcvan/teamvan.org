# Competitions

## This Season

We expect 15 to 20 committed members this season. We plan to compete at the Orlando Regional and the Rocket City Regional, and at the FIRST Championship if we qualify.

## How Qualifying for the FIRST Championship Works

Team V.A.N. is in a Regional-style state, which means our registration covers one regional event rather than a district season. We qualify for the FIRST Championship directly out of a regional by placing well enough in the event's rankings and playoffs, or by winning certain awards that carry an automatic bid, such as the Regional Impact Award or an Engineering Inspiration Award.

This season we'll be registered for two regionals, the Orlando Regional and the Rocket City Regional, so we get two chances to qualify.
