# Outreach

<Quip>
  Robots are the excuse. People are the point.
</Quip>

For us, outreach means bringing FIRST to southern Brevard and Indian River County, where students have little access to FRC: hosting tours at competitions, mentoring younger FIRST LEGO League (FLL) teams, and talking with local leaders about what hands-on robotics can do for students here.

## Community Leadership & Advocacy

### Space Coast Showdown

On September 26, 2026, our team volunteered at the Space Coast Showdown. In addition to running field reset during the morning, our members served as ambassadors for Team V.A.N. and FIRST Florida, talking with NASA employees, Eastern Florida State College (EFSC) directors, and school board candidates.

Team V.A.N. also hosted guided tours for community leaders, educational advocates, and industry professionals. We talked about what it's like to be a rookie FRC team and how hands-on STEM through FIRST reaches students across Brevard. We spoke with:

- **Tammy Dennis** (Former Brevard District 5 School Board candidate) — Toured our setup, met most of the Brevard teams at the event, and expressed strong support for FIRST and for expanding robotics programs in Brevard County schools.
- **Gordon B. Coffey** (NASA mechanical engineer) — Spent time with the Florida teams' robots and offered to connect Team V.A.N. with engineering networks across Florida, including contacts at aerospace companies such as Lockheed Martin.
- **Daniel Mondshein** (Collegewide Chair, Eastern Florida State College) — Toured with our members and offered to open EFSC resources to the team where possible, supporting our students' technical development and paths into college STEM programs.

### West Shore FLL Scrimmage

In October 2026, Team V.A.N. volunteered at an FLL scrimmage in Brevard County. Our members served as referees and judges, and helped with setup and teardown so the event ran smoothly.

<Figure
    src="/src/images/pages/west-shore-fll-scrimmage.jpg"
    alt="Students gathered around an FLL robotics field at the West Shore FLL Scrimmage"
    variant="wide"
/>

We were impressed by how much these students had prepared for the scrimmage in just a couple of months. It was great to see several teams we've mentored do well. We're proud to support the next generation of robotics students and help grow STEM opportunities in our community, and we wish all of these teams good luck at their qualifiers!

## Start an FLL Team

Our area has plenty of students who would join an FLL team if one existed near them. If you are thinking about starting one, we wrote up [how to get a team running](/fll/), and we are happy to help.
