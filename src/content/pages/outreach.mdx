# Outreach

Team V.A.N. brings FIRST programs to southern Brevard and Indian River County, where students have little access to FRC. Our outreach includes tours at competitions, mentoring FIRST LEGO League (FLL) teams, and meetings with local leaders about hands-on robotics in local schools.

## Community Leadership & Advocacy

### Space Coast Showdown

On September 26, 2026, team members volunteered at the Space Coast Showdown. We ran field reset in the morning and represented Team V.A.N. and FIRST Florida, speaking with NASA employees, Eastern Florida State College (EFSC) directors, and school board candidates.

The team also gave guided tours to community leaders, educators, and industry professionals. We described our first season as an FRC team and how FIRST programs reach students across Brevard. We met with:

- **Tammy Dennis** (former Brevard District 5 School Board candidate): Toured our setup, met most of the Brevard teams at the event, and expressed support for FIRST and for expanding robotics programs in Brevard County schools.
- **Gordon B. Coffey** (NASA mechanical engineer): Spent time with the Florida teams' robots and offered to connect Team V.A.N. with engineering networks across Florida, including contacts at aerospace companies such as Lockheed Martin.
- **Daniel Mondshein** (Collegewide Chair, Eastern Florida State College): Toured with our members and offered to open EFSC resources to the team where possible, including support for students' technical development and college STEM paths.

### West Shore FLL Scrimmage

In October 2026, team members volunteered at an FLL scrimmage in Brevard County. We served as referees and judges and helped with setup and teardown.
