# Outreach

For us, outreach means introducing the program itself to a part of Florida that doesn't have an established FRC presence yet: hosting tours at competitions, mentoring younger FIRST LEGO League (FLL) teams, and talking with local leaders about what hands-on robotics can do for students here.

## Community Leadership & Advocacy

<Collapse title="Space Coast Showdown">
  Our team had an extremely successful day on September 26th volunteering at the Space Coast Showdown! In addition to keeping matches running smoothly as field resetters in the morning hours of the competition, our members proudly served as ambassadors for Team V.A.N. and FIRST Florida, engaging with NASA employees, Eastern Florida State College (EFSC) directors, and school board member candidates.

  At the Space Coast Showdown, Team V.A.N. invited and hosted guided tours for community leaders, educational advocates, and industry professionals. We talked about what it's like to be a rookie FRC team and how hands-on STEM through FIRST reaches local youth across Brevard:

  <Figure src="/images/pages/tammy-dennis.jpeg" alt="Team V.A.N. members talking with Tammy Dennis at the Space Coast Showdown" caption="Team V.A.N. members introduce District 5 school board candidate Tammy Dennis to the FIRST Robotics Competition program and show her the competition at Space Coast Showdown on Saturday, Sept. 26." variant="natural" />

  * **Tammy Dennis** (Brevard District 5 School Board Candidate) — Toured our setup and expressed strong support for the FIRST vision and the expansion of robotics programs across Brevard County schools. Viewed and interacted with most Brevard teams.
  * **Gordon B. Coffey** (NASA Mechanical Engineer) — Enthusiastically viewed Florida teams' robots and offered to connect Team V.A.N. with professional engineering networks across Florida, including contacts at aerospace leaders like Lockheed Martin.
  * **Daniel Mondshein** (Eastern Florida State College Collegewide Chair) — Toured with our members and offered full access to EFSC resources, to the best of his ability, to support our students' technical development and post-secondary STEM pathways.
</Collapse>

<Collapse title="West Shore FLL Scrimmage">
  This October, Team V.A.N. enjoyed volunteering to help run an FLL scrimmage in Brevard County. Our members served as referees and judges while helping with event setup and teardown to make sure everything ran smoothly.

  <Figure src="/images/pages/west-shore-fll-scrimmage.jpg" alt="Students gathered around an FLL robotics field at the West Shore FLL Scrimmage" variant="wide" />

  We were so impressed by how much these elementary school students were able to prepare for competition in just a couple months. It was heartwarming to see several teams we’ve mentored succeeding at competition. We’re proud to support the next generation of robotics students and help grow STEM opportunities in our community. We wish all of these teams good luck at qualifiers!
</Collapse>
