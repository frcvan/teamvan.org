Team V.A.N. is FRC team 12148, a first-year community team in Palm Bay, Florida. Our students come from southern Brevard and Indian River counties, and they run the team themselves. We work with The MindForge Guild, the 501(c)(3) makerspace that hosts us.

We started the team because the nearest FRC team is more than a 40-minute drive from southern Brevard, and Indian River County has none at all. We wanted students here to have a team they could reach without a long commute.

## Join Us

Not every job on the team involves a wrench. Sponsorships, the budget, photos, video, and the presentation judges see all need someone to do them. No experience is required for any of it, and the students who take those jobs are as much a part of the team as the ones building the robot.
