Founded in 2026, Team V.A.N. (FRC 12148) is a student managed, rookie FIRST Robotics Competition team.\
Based in Palm Bay, with our main build space at Mind Forge Guild, and support from Eastern Florida State College, we serve students in southern Brevard and Indian River Counties.\
We are the only FRC community team in Brevard County. And are closest FRC team to the Sebastian River, Vero Beach and Indian River Charter High Schools.

## Mission

The reason for our initiative is limited access to STEM extracurriculars in southern Brevard and Indian River Counties. \
Indian River County currently has no FRC teams, and students in southern Brevard often have to travel 40+ minutes to join an existing program.

## Join Us

We are always looking for passionate students to join our team. \
We focus on multi-disciplinary education, whether you're interested in engineering, business operations, or media, you would make an impact! No matter your experience.
