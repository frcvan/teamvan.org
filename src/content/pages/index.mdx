## Our Mission

<Coverage />

We started Team V.A.N. because a team you can't reach isn't really an option. Students from those five schools can build, program, and run the team without leaving the area.

## Join Us

No experience required, and not every job involves a wrench. A team also runs on sponsors, budgets, video, graphics, and the people who explain it to judges.
