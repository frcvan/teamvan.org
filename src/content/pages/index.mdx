Team V.A.N. is FRC team 12148, a first-year community team in Palm Bay, Florida. We run it like a small business: students set the budget, pitch sponsors, plan events, run the social accounts, and build the robot.

Our students come from southern Brevard and Indian River counties, and we work with The MindForge Guild, the 501(c)(3) makerspace that hosts us.

We started the team because the nearest FRC team is more than a 40-minute drive from southern Brevard, and Indian River County has none at all. We wanted students here to have a team they could reach without a long commute.

## Join the team

The team needs students for partnerships, finance, media, and operations. You can pitch sponsors, set a budget, plan events, run the social accounts, take photos and video, and present the team to judges. No experience is required, and you do not need to be an engineer or want a STEM career.
