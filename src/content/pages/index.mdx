## Who We Are

We started Team V.A.N. because a team you can't reach isn't really an option. Indian River County has no FRC team at all, and the closest one to southern Brevard is more than a 40-minute drive. That is the gap we are trying to close.

<Coverage />

The team draws students from five schools: Palm Bay, Bayside, Sebastian River, Vero Beach, and Indian River Charter. Twenty students from those schools have said they are interested, and they run the team themselves, from the build and the budget to the presentation judges see.

## Join Us

Not every job on the team involves a wrench. Sponsorships, the budget, photos, video, and the presentation judges see all need someone to do them. No experience is required for any of it, and the students who take those jobs are as much a part of the team as the ones building the robot.
