# For Sponsors

## Partner with a First-Year Team

Team V.A.N. is a first-year FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) team, so we are still building out our organization. We can put together a sponsorship that fits yours, whether that means branding, a team showcase at an event, or something else you already have in mind.

**Anything helps.** We operate in partnership with [The MindForge Guild](https://themindforgeguild.org), the 501(c)(3) makerspace that hosts our team, so sponsorships and donations are made to the Guild on our behalf.

## Why FIRST Robotics Is Worth Backing

FRC has a long record of measurable outcomes for students who might not otherwise have access to hands-on engineering. Brandeis University's 10-year FIRST Longitudinal Study, which compared FIRST alumni with similar students who did not participate in FIRST, found that:

- About 90% of FIRST alumni hold a college degree, compared with 55% nationally.
- 83% major in a STEM field by their fourth year of college, compared with 61% of the comparison group.
- 63% work in a STEM field, compared with 42% of the comparison group.

FIRST participants can also apply for $80 million in scholarships from more than 200 colleges, universities, and other providers.

Sponsor support is what makes a team possible for students in southern Brevard and Indian River County, where there is currently little or no local access to an FRC program.
