# For Sponsors

## Partner with a First-Year Team

Team V.A.N. is a first-year FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) team still putting structures in place. We can work with you on a sponsorship that fits your organization, whether that means branding, a team showcase at an event, or another community initiative you already have in mind.

**Anything helps.** We operate in partnership with [The Mind Forge Guild](https://themindforgeguild.org), the 501(c)(3) makerspace that hosts our team, so sponsorships and donations are made to them on our behalf.

## Why FIRST Robotics Is Worth Backing

FRC is a program with a long track record of measurable outcomes for students who might not otherwise have access to hands-on engineering. Nationally, FIRST alumni report:

* \~90% college degree attainment, compared with roughly 55% nationally, according to Brandeis University's 10-year FIRST Longitudinal Study.
* 83% declare a STEM major by their fourth year of college.
* 63% are currently employed in a STEM field.
* Access to 200+ scholarship, university, and workforce partners offering exclusive scholarship opportunities to FIRST participants.

Sponsor support turns those outcomes into opportunities for students in Palm Bay and Indian River County who currently have little or no local access to an FRC program.
