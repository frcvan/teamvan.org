# Join Team V.A.N.

Team V.A.N. is a student-managed FIRST Robotics Competition team based at The MindForge Guild in Palm Bay. We draw students from southern Brevard and Indian River counties. Some build the robot; others run partnerships, finance, media, operations, and speaking.

## Requirements

- You can attend meetings and spend four afternoons a week on robotics.
- No prior robotics experience is required.

You do not have to build the robot or want a STEM career. The team also needs students for partnerships, finance, media, operations, and speaking.

## Where we meet

We meet at The MindForge Guild, 2475 Palm Bay Rd, Suite 210, in Palm Bay. The Guild provides the office space and contractor bays where we build and prototype. Every meeting and event is posted on the [calendar](/calendar/) at least a week ahead.

## What you can work on

Students run the budget, sponsorships, media, and events alongside the robot. You can join partnerships, finance, media, operations, speaking, or build, and no department requires prior experience.

## Cost

The $100 participation fee is lower than most other robotics teams charge. It goes directly into team shirts, team insurance, and training sessions. The fee is due after you've joined the team - nothing is due with the interest form. If paying it is a problem, [contact us](mailto:contact@teamvan.org). We want to be as accommodating as possible.

The $100 fee is the only required cost to be on the team. We will not ask for any extra money. Competitions are optional.

Rocket City in Huntsville is optional. The trip could cost less than $1,000, but the final number depends on travel and lodging and may be higher or lower.

Transportation to Orlando is not provided, but all students are welcome to join us at the competition for free.

All fundraising goes toward the students and the robots. Every dollar raised lowers what parents pay.

## How to join

Fill out the interest form and email us if you have questions. We will follow up with the next meeting date and what to expect.
