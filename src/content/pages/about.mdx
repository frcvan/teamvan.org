# What Is FIRST Robotics?

The FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) is an international high school engineering program. Each January, teams receive a Kit of Parts and a new game, then have about six weeks to design, build, program, and test a competition robot before their first event. From February through April, teams compete in three-team alliance matches at regional and district events around the world, working to qualify for the FIRST Championship.

Two values matter in FRC as much as the robots do: **Gracious Professionalism**, which means treating competitors with the same respect as teammates, and **Coopertition**, which means teams can help each other and still compete hard. Teams also earn judged awards for engineering quality, community impact, and mentorship, not just match wins.
