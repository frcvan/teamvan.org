# What Is FIRST Robotics?

The FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) is an international high school engineering program. Every January, teams get a Kit of Parts and a new game, then have about six weeks to design, build, program, and test a competition robot before shipping it off to events. From February through April, teams compete in head-to-head matches at regional and district events around the world, working to qualify for the FIRST Championship.

FRC runs on two values as much as it runs on robots: **Gracious Professionalism**, treating competitors with the same respect as teammates, and **Coopertition**, the idea that teams can help each other and still compete hard. Teams also earn judged awards for engineering quality, community impact, and mentorship, not just match wins.

## Our Season at a Glance

Team V.A.N. runs on the standard FIRST Robotics Competition calendar, with a team kickoff of our own on November 15 to get new members ready before the season starts. Exact dates shift from year to year; this is the shape of a typical season.
