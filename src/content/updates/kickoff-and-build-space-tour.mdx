We held two kickoffs: one on Tuesday, November 10, and one on Sunday, November 15. About 10 students came on Tuesday and about 20 on Sunday, for about 30 across both dates.

The group was a mix of experience levels, with many students new to robotics, and most were eighth graders and juniors.

We described the leadership roles and opportunities on the team, along with the time commitment and the season budget. The MindForge Guild hosts showed us around their office space and the contractor bays where we can build and prototype physical solutions.

We do not have a date for our next meeting yet because of flooding and Thanksgiving break. We will plan one in the next week, and every meeting and event will be on the [calendar](/calendar/) at least a week ahead.

If you are interested, fill out the [interest form](https://tally.so/r/KYbz6k) and email [contact@teamvan.org](mailto:contact@teamvan.org). We are really excited for the future of this team.
